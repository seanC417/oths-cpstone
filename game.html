Chapter One: The Confiscated Phone

It is Friday afternoon, and you are sitting in class when the principal catches you using your phone. Your phone is taken away and brought to the principal's office.

The principal tells you that the only way to get your phone back is for one of your parents to come to school and pick it up. The problem is that you do not want your parents to know that you got in trouble.

After the final bell rings, most students and teachers leave campus. You stand in the hallway thinking about what to do.

What will you do?
